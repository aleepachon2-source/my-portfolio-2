Vidamia is a lifestyle brand I designed and built end-to-end on Shopify as part of my MBA's e-commerce module — from information architecture and product catalogue structure to visual merchandising and the full customer shopping journey.

The goal wasn't just to launch a store. It was to understand how every design decision — a navigation label, a product layout, a hero image — either removes or adds friction in the path from discovery to purchase.

"Design the feeling first. The conversion follows."

The Challenge

Shopify makes it easy to open a store. It doesn't make it easy to build one that feels intentional. The challenge was to go beyond the default theme and create a brand experience that communicates personality at every touchpoint — from the hero banner to the product page copy — while keeping the UX clean enough that a customer on mobile can get from discovery to checkout with no friction.

Design & Merchandising Decisions
01

Theme selection with intention. Chose a minimal, grid-forward theme that lets product photography drive the brand feeling — not the theme's decorative elements. Customized typography and color palette to match Vidamia's warm, lifestyle-oriented identity.

02

Visual merchandising as strategy. Applied retail merchandising principles digitally: anchor with hero products, group complementary items, surface bestsellers first, and use white space to signal quality rather than fill every pixel.

03

Copy tone as brand voice. Product descriptions written to feel warm and personal — not feature-list-heavy. The copy reinforces the brand promise: "live beautifully, every day."

04

Collection architecture for SEO and UX. Collections designed with two goals: human browsing logic (Shop by Mood, Shop by Room) and search-friendly naming for organic discovery.

Key Learnings

Placement is strategy. Where you put a product on a page is a merchandising decision, not a layout decision. The first product a customer sees sets expectations for everything below it.

Mobile-first reveals the essential. Designing at 375px forces you to prioritize ruthlessly. If it doesn't fit on mobile, it probably wasn't necessary on desktop either.

Shopify's constraints are features. Working within Shopify's theme architecture (Liquid, sections, metafields) teaches you to solve problems creatively within a system — a skill that transfers to any platform.

Brand voice lives in the details. The difference between a generic store and a memorable one is often just copy tone, color temperature, and whitespace discipline.
